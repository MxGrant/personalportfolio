About me

A developer who cares how it feels.

I'm a Computer Science graduate who likes the space where logic meets craft: building things that work reliably underneath and feel effortless on the surface.

Most of what I know has come from making things — individual builds where I owned every decision, and collaborative projects where the result depended on communication, planning and shared standards. I've worked with Agile and Scrum-style workflows, and I'm comfortable turning a loose brief into a plan, a prototype and then a finished product.

On the design side I'm drawn to UI/UX: wireframing, prototyping and checking whether real people can find their way around what I've built. On the engineering side I care about clean structure, accessible markup and code that someone else can pick up without a map.

I'm always interested in work where design and engineering have to solve a real problem together — that's the part I enjoy most.

  • → Interest in UI/UX, web design and software development
  • → Experience across individual and collaborative projects
  • → Comfortable with Agile methodologies and project management
